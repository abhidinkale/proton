# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** `[DATE]` · **Version:** `[VERSION]`

### The short version

We are `[COMPANY NAME]`. We make a health app called `[APP NAME]`.

- We collect only what the app needs to work.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not show ads or use advertising trackers.
- Your health records stay on your phone unless you choose to send them somewhere.
- You can ask us what we hold, get a copy, or have it deleted.

The rest of this page explains that in more detail.

---

## 1. What this app is

`[APP NAME]` helps you keep track of your health: your medications, appointments, test results, and messages from your care team. It also has an assistant that can explain your records in plain language.

It does **not** replace your doctor. It cannot diagnose you, and it cannot prescribe anything.

---

## 2. What we collect

**Things you give us**

| What | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| Account details | Your email address, and a password that is stored only on your phone in a scrambled form |
| Your health information | Conditions, allergies, medications, test results, appointments |
| Things you write or upload | Messages to your care team, photos of documents, notes |
| Your date of birth and sex | Used to show your age and to flag health information that depends on it |

**Things the app collects on its own**

- Basic device information needed to run the app (operating system version, app version).
- Crash information, if you have crash reporting turned on. `[PLACEHOLDER: confirm whether crash reporting is enabled — it is currently not]`
- **Not** your location, contacts, or photos, unless you pick a photo to upload.

**Things we do not collect**

- No advertising ID.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No access to your microphone or camera without you starting that feature yourself. The app asks permission each time.

---

## 3. Why we collect it

We use your information to:

1. Show you your own health record.
2. Let your care team send you messages and see the information you have agreed to share.
3. Remind you to take medication, if you turn reminders on.
4. Explain your records to you when you ask the assistant a question.
5. Keep the app working and fix bugs.

We do **not** use your health information to train AI models. We do not sell it. We do not use it to decide what ads you see, because there are no ads.

---

## 4. Where your information goes

**By default: nowhere.** The app stores your information on your phone, in storage that other apps cannot read.

It leaves your phone only when:

- **You use the assistant.** Your question, plus the part of your record needed to answer it, is sent to an AI provider so it can write a reply. `[PLACEHOLDER: name the current AI provider and its country once this is live.]`
- **You message your care team.** The message goes to the clinic's system.
- **You book an appointment.** The request goes to the clinic.
- **You export or share something yourself.** For example, sending a PDF to your email.

We do not run a background sync. Nothing is uploaded while you are not using the app.

## 7. Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app: **Settings → Account → Delete account**.

When you do:

1. Your data is removed from your phone immediately.
2. We delete your data from our systems within `[30]` days.
3. Backup copies disappear within `[90]` days.
4. We keep only what the law says we must — for example, a record that a prescription was issued. We will tell you what we kept and why.

If you cannot use the in-app option, email `[PRIVACY EMAIL]` and we will do it for you.
