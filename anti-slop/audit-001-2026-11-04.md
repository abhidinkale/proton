## Numbered Findings List

### 1. R-02 (Copywriting) — Em Dash (`—`) in UI Strings
- **File:** `apps/proton-desktop/src/main.cjs:339`, `apps/web/src/design-system/theme/PaletteSwitcher.tsx:64`, `apps/web/src/pages/Billing.tsx:217`
- **Priority:** **HIGH** (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Em dashes (`—`) are a primary AI generation tell in interface copy; prose should use commas, colons, or parentheses.
- **Proposed Fix:** Replace `Proton Clinic — Desktop Operating System` with `Proton Clinic: Desktop Operating System`, and replace em dashes in UI strings with colons or commas.

### 2. R-26 (Interactive Elements) — Dead & Misrouted Navigation Links in Sidebar
- **File:** `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/Sidebar.tsx:29-33`, `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/Sidebar.tsx:96-101`
- **Priority:** **HIGH** (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Nav items "Clinical Guidelines" and "Clinic Operations" point to generic `/documents` and `/settings` without unique destination views, and the bottom AI orb has `cursor-pointer` but zero click behavior.
- **Proposed Fix:** Connect the AI orb to open the Proton Copilot assistant drawer, and route secondary navigation items to dedicated functional panels (or remove unbuilt dummy items).

### 3. R-32 (Keyboard Accessibility) & R-39 (Semantics) — Missing Accessible Names on Icon-Only Sidebar Rails
- **File:** `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/Sidebar.tsx:47-66`
- **Priority:** **HIGH** (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Sidebar icon-only navigation links render only SVG icons without explicit `aria-label` or visually-hidden labels, failing screen-reader accessibility and keyboard inspection.
- **Proposed Fix:** Add `aria-label={item.label}` and `aria-hidden="true"` on inner Lucide icons across all sidebar links.

### 4. R-32 (Keyboard Accessibility) — Composer Textarea Stripped Focus Outline
- **File:** `apps/web/src/pages/Assistant.tsx:583`
- **Priority:** **HIGH** (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** `outline-none` removes the native browser focus indicator without an explicit high-contrast keyboard focus ring.
- **Proposed Fix:** Add `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-amber-500/40 focus-visible:outline-none` on the textarea.

### 5. R-40 (Performance & Semantics) — Missing Intrinsic Image Dimensions
- **File:** `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/Sidebar.tsx:75-79`
- **Priority:** **HIGH** (Hard Gate)
- **Reason:** Caduceus logo image tag `<img src="/assets/caduceus-gold.png" />` lacks explicit `width` and `height` attributes, risking layout shifts.
- **Proposed Fix:** Provide explicit `width={28} height={28}` attributes on the emblem element.

### 6. R-01 (Color & Gradients) & R-04 (Icons) — Generic AI Radial Gradient Orb
- **File:** `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/Sidebar.tsx:96-101`
- **Priority:** **MEDIUM** (Purpose-Gate)
- **Reason:** The pink-to-indigo-to-cyan pulsing radial orb at the bottom of the sidebar is a classic AI slop aesthetic with no clinical diagnostic purpose.
- **Proposed Fix:** Replace the decorative rainbow sphere with a functional, restrained Clinical Status indicator (e.g. green/amber Medplum database synchronization pill or clean physician presence indicator).

### 7. R-10 (Glassmorphism) — Excessive Backdrop Blur Stacking
- **File:** `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/AppShell.tsx:204`, `apps/web/src/pages/Assistant.tsx:409`, `apps/web/src/pages/Assistant.tsx:485`, `apps/web/src/pages/Assistant.tsx:563`
- **Priority:** **MEDIUM** (Purpose-Gate)
- **Reason:** `backdrop-blur-2xl` is stacked across the main canvas, the top bar, message bubbles, and the composer simultaneously, exceeding the dose cap (max 1-2 elements) and washing out depth.
- **Proposed Fix:** Keep subtle blur on the floating composer dock and top bar, while using solid, crisp dark surfaces (`#0c0e12`, `#0f1217`) with refined hairline borders for message cards and containers.

### 8. R-13 (Glow) — Overuse of Glowing Box Shadows
- **File:** `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/Sidebar.tsx:53`, `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/Sidebar.tsx:100`
- **Priority:** **MEDIUM** (Purpose-Gate)
- **Reason:** Multiple active glows (`shadow-[0_0_12px_rgba(245,158,11,0.15)]`, orb glow, pulse rings) reduce contrast and read as generative decoration rather than intentional clinical focus.
- **Proposed Fix:** Reserve subtle glow exclusively for critical patient alerts or active attention states, keeping standard navigation and UI controls crisp and matte.

### 9. R-09 (Badges) & R-16 (Buzzwords) — Redundant AI Capsule Badges
- **File:** `apps/web/src/pages/Assistant.tsx:424-428`
- **Priority:** **MEDIUM** (Purpose-Gate)
- **Reason:** Stacking multiple capsule badges (`GEMINI 3.5 FLASH LITE`, `FHIR R4`) in the header title reads like AI feature decoration rather than a purposeful clinical tool indicator.
- **Proposed Fix:** Consolidate into a single restrained, high-legibility clinical engine tag (`EHR Copilot v2.4 · FHIR R4`) without artificial pill borders and neon glow.

### 10. R-11 (Border Radius) — Over-Uniform Rounded Pill Shapes
- **File:** `apps/web/src/pages/Assistant.tsx:550-558`, `apps/web/src/design-system/layout/TopBar.tsx:64-92`
- **Priority:** **LOW** (Quality Locks)
- **Reason:** Quick command chips, action buttons, search bars, and tags all use identical rounded-xl / pill geometry, flattening the distinction between interactive buttons, inputs, and static chips.
- **Proposed Fix:** Implement a disciplined radius scale: `rounded-lg` (8px) for inputs/search, `rounded-xl` (12px) for primary action cards, and `rounded-md` (6px) for badges and chips.
