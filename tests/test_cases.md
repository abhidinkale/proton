### Case 1: Normal English (General Checkup)
**Doctor:** Good morning. What brings you in today?
**Patient:** I've been having this persistent dry cough for the last two weeks. No fever, but my throat feels scratchy. I took some over-the-counter cough syrup but it didn't help much.
**Doctor:** Any history of asthma or recent travel?
**Patient:** No, none of that. I am allergic to penicillin though.
**Doctor:** Let's prescribe you a course of anti-allergic medication, Cetirizine 10mg once a night for 5 days. Keep drinking warm water. Follow up if it doesn't resolve in a week.

### Case 2: Indian English (Fever & Body ache)
**Doctor:** Hello, tell me, what is the problem?
**Patient:** Doctor, I am having full body pain and fever since yesterday night. Temperature was 101 today morning. I also feel very weak.
**Doctor:** Did you take any paracetamol?
**Patient:** Yes, I took one Dolo 650 in the morning, but fever came back after 4 hours.
**Doctor:** Okay, any loose motions or vomiting?
**Patient:** No sir.
**Doctor:** It looks like a viral fever. Continue Dolo 650 SOS for fever. Take plenty of fluids. I will also write a Vitamin C supplement. Come back after 3 days if fever persists.

### Case 3: Hinglish / Code-switching (Gastritis)

### Case 4: Short Consultation (Prescription Refill)

### Case 5: Longer Consultation (Chronic Back Pain & Hypertension)
**Doctor:** How has your back pain been since the last visit?
**Patient:** It's slightly better, but the lower back still hurts when I sit for too long. Especially at work. It shoots down my left leg sometimes.

**Doctor:** We might need to adjust the dose if it stays high, but let's monitor for now. For the back, I'm prescribing Etoricoxib 90mg for 5 days. You MUST restart the physiotherapy. Also, I'll recommend an MRI of the lumbar spine if the radiating pain doesn't stop.
